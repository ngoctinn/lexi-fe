# Requirements Document: AI Conversation Quality Improvement

## Introduction

This document specifies requirements for improving the conversational AI quality in Lexi, a language learning application for Vietnamese learners practicing English conversation. The current AI responses are robotic, too long, not beginner-friendly, and lack natural conversation flow. This feature will migrate from Claude 3 Haiku to Amazon Nova Pro with optimized prompt engineering to deliver natural, encouraging, and level-appropriate conversational experiences.

## Glossary

- **AI_Agent**: The conversational AI system that generates responses to learner inputs
- **Conversation_Engine**: The service that orchestrates AI response generation, including prompt construction and model invocation
- **Response_Generator**: The component responsible for calling the LLM and formatting responses
- **Persona_System**: The identity and personality configuration for the AI conversation partner
- **Scaffolding_System**: The progressive hint system that helps beginners when they struggle
- **Feedback_Formatter**: The component that structures post-session evaluation feedback
- **TTS_Engine**: Text-to-speech service that converts AI responses to spoken audio
- **Learner**: The Vietnamese user practicing English conversation (proficiency levels A1-C2)
- **Session**: A single conversation practice instance with defined scenario, roles, and goals
- **Turn**: A single exchange in the conversation (learner speaks, AI responds)
- **Proficiency_Level**: CEFR levels A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2 indicating English skill
- **Response_Token**: A unit of text generated by the LLM (approximately 4 characters)
- **TTFT**: Time To First Token - latency from request to first response character
- **Bilingual_Hint**: A support message containing both Vietnamese and English text
- **Delivery_Cue**: Emotional or tonal instruction embedded in response for natural TTS

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Natural AI Persona Design

**User Story:** As a learner, I want the AI to feel like a friendly conversation partner, so that I feel comfortable practicing English without judgment.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Persona_System SHALL define a clear identity including role, personality traits, and conversational style
2. THE Persona_System SHALL adapt emotional tone based on learner proficiency level (encouraging for A1-A2, supportive for B1-B2, engaging for C1-C2)
3. THE Persona_System SHALL maintain consistent character throughout the session based on the assigned AI role
4. WHEN the learner shows frustration or difficulty, THE AI_Agent SHALL respond with encouraging language and emotional warmth
5. THE AI_Agent SHALL NOT reveal its artificial nature unless directly asked by the learner

### Requirement 2: Dynamic Response Length by Proficiency Level

**User Story:** As a beginner learner, I want shorter AI responses, so that I can understand and process the conversation without feeling overwhelmed.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the proficiency level is A1, THE Response_Generator SHALL limit responses to 40 tokens maximum
2. WHEN the proficiency level is A2, THE Response_Generator SHALL limit responses to 60 tokens maximum
3. WHEN the proficiency level is B1, THE Response_Generator SHALL limit responses to 100 tokens maximum
4. WHEN the proficiency level is B2, THE Response_Generator SHALL limit responses to 150 tokens maximum
5. WHEN the proficiency level is C1, THE Response_Generator SHALL limit responses to 200 tokens maximum
6. WHEN the proficiency level is C2, THE Response_Generator SHALL limit responses to 250 tokens maximum
7. THE Response_Generator SHALL enforce token limits through LLM configuration parameters

### Requirement 3: Spoken-First Response Format

**User Story:** As a learner, I want AI responses that sound natural when spoken aloud, so that the conversation feels like talking to a real person.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Response_Generator SHALL produce responses without markdown formatting (no asterisks, bullets, or headers)
2. THE Response_Generator SHALL produce responses without structured lists or numbered items
3. THE Response_Generator SHALL include delivery cues for emotional expression (e.g., "[warmly]", "[encouragingly]")
4. THE Response_Generator SHALL limit responses to one question per turn
5. THE Response_Generator SHALL use conversational sentence structures suitable for spoken dialogue
6. THE Response_Generator SHALL avoid written-only constructs like parenthetical asides or em-dashes

### Requirement 4: Bilingual Scaffolding for Beginners

**User Story:** As an A1 or A2 learner, I want hints in Vietnamese when I struggle, so that I can continue the conversation without getting stuck.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the proficiency level is A1 or A2, THE Scaffolding_System SHALL provide bilingual hints containing both Vietnamese and English text
2. WHEN a learner remains silent for 10 seconds, THE Scaffolding_System SHALL provide a gentle prompt in English
3. WHEN a learner remains silent for 20 seconds, THE Scaffolding_System SHALL provide a vocabulary hint as a bilingual hint
4. WHEN a learner remains silent for 30 seconds, THE Scaffolding_System SHALL provide a sentence starter as a bilingual hint
5. WHEN the proficiency level is B1 or higher, THE Scaffolding_System SHALL provide hints in English only
6. THE Scaffolding_System SHALL format bilingual hints with Vietnamese first, followed by English translation

### Requirement 5: Structured Post-Session Feedback

**User Story:** As a learner, I want detailed feedback after my session, so that I understand my strengths and areas for improvement.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Feedback_Formatter SHALL structure feedback into four categories: fluency, grammar, vocabulary, and overall performance
2. THE Feedback_Formatter SHALL assign a numeric score (0-100) to each category
3. THE Feedback_Formatter SHALL include an emoji indicator for each category (🟢 for 80+, 🟡 for 60-79, 🔴 for below 60)
4. THE Feedback_Formatter SHALL provide specific examples of learner mistakes with corrections
5. THE Feedback_Formatter SHALL provide 2-3 actionable next steps for improvement
6. THE Feedback_Formatter SHALL deliver all feedback text in Vietnamese
7. THE Feedback_Formatter SHALL limit feedback to 200 words maximum

### Requirement 6: Amazon Nova Pro Model Migration

**User Story:** As a system administrator, I want to migrate from Claude 3 Haiku to Amazon Nova Pro, so that we can leverage improved conversational capabilities and cost efficiency.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL invoke Amazon Nova Pro model (model ID: "us.amazon.nova-pro-v1:0")
2. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL configure the model with temperature parameter between 0.7 and 0.9 for natural variation
3. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL configure the model with top_p parameter of 0.9 for response diversity
4. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL pass system prompts using Nova Pro's native prompt format
5. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL handle Nova Pro API responses and extract generated text
6. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL maintain backward compatibility with existing session data structures

### Requirement 7: Latency Optimization for Real-Time Conversation

**User Story:** As a learner, I want fast AI responses, so that the conversation feels natural and real-time.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Response_Generator SHALL achieve Time To First Token (TTFT) of less than 400 milliseconds for 95% of requests
2. THE Response_Generator SHALL enable streaming mode for progressive response delivery
3. THE Response_Generator SHALL configure model inference parameters to prioritize latency over maximum quality
4. WHEN streaming is enabled, THE Response_Generator SHALL deliver response tokens to the TTS_Engine incrementally
5. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL implement request timeout of 5 seconds maximum
6. WHEN a request exceeds timeout, THE Conversation_Engine SHALL return a fallback response

### Requirement 8: Prompt Structure Optimization

**User Story:** As a system administrator, I want a well-structured prompt template, so that the AI generates consistent, high-quality responses.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL structure prompts in five sections: Identity, Personality, Behaviors, Response Rules, and Guardrails
2. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL define Identity section with role name, relationship to learner, and primary purpose
3. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL define Personality section with 3-5 character traits and emotional tone
4. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL define Behaviors section with conversational patterns and interaction style
5. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL define Response Rules section with format constraints and length limits
6. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL define Guardrails section with transparency rules, scope boundaries, and prohibited behaviors
7. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL inject proficiency-level-specific instructions into the Response Rules section

### Requirement 9: Conversational Guardrails

**User Story:** As a learner, I want the AI to stay focused on language learning, so that I get maximum value from my practice time.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the learner goes off-topic, THE AI_Agent SHALL gently redirect to the session scenario within 1 turn
2. WHEN the learner uses inappropriate language, THE AI_Agent SHALL acknowledge professionally and redirect to learning goals
3. WHEN the learner writes in Vietnamese, THE AI_Agent SHALL request English and provide a simple English prompt
4. WHEN the learner asks about non-learning topics, THE AI_Agent SHALL acknowledge briefly and return to the scenario
5. THE AI_Agent SHALL NOT fabricate information about the scenario context or learner's background
6. THE AI_Agent SHALL NOT provide grammar corrections during conversation (corrections happen in post-session feedback only)

### Requirement 10: Context Window Management

**User Story:** As a system administrator, I want efficient conversation history management, so that the system remains performant during long sessions.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL maintain a sliding window of the 10 most recent turns
2. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL include only the sliding window turns in the LLM context
3. WHEN turn history exceeds 10 turns, THE Conversation_Engine SHALL remove the oldest turns from context
4. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL preserve the complete turn history in the database regardless of context window size
5. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL include session metadata (scenario, roles, goals, level) in every LLM request

### Requirement 11: Response Quality Metrics

**User Story:** As a product manager, I want to measure response quality, so that I can track improvement over time.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL log response latency (TTFT and total generation time) for every turn
2. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL log response token count for every turn
3. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL log model invocation success/failure status for every turn
4. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL calculate and store average response latency per session
5. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL calculate and store average response length per session
6. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL expose metrics through CloudWatch for monitoring and alerting

### Requirement 12: Emotional Delivery Cues for TTS

**User Story:** As a learner, I want the AI's voice to sound natural and expressive, so that the conversation feels engaging and human-like.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Response_Generator SHALL embed delivery cues in responses using square bracket notation (e.g., "[warmly]", "[encouragingly]")
2. THE Response_Generator SHALL include delivery cues at the beginning of responses when emotional tone is important
3. THE Response_Generator SHALL use delivery cues for: warmly, encouragingly, enthusiastically, gently, thoughtfully, curiously
4. THE TTS_Engine SHALL interpret delivery cues and adjust prosody accordingly
5. THE Response_Generator SHALL limit delivery cues to one per response to avoid over-annotation

### Requirement 13: Progressive Hint System Implementation

**User Story:** As a beginner learner, I want progressive hints when I'm stuck, so that I can learn to construct sentences independently.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Scaffolding_System SHALL implement three hint levels: gentle prompt, vocabulary hint, sentence starter
2. WHEN providing a gentle prompt, THE Scaffolding_System SHALL ask an encouraging question related to the conversation topic
3. WHEN providing a vocabulary hint, THE Scaffolding_System SHALL suggest 2-3 relevant words as a bilingual hint
4. WHEN providing a sentence starter, THE Scaffolding_System SHALL provide the first 3-5 words of a valid response as a bilingual hint
5. THE Scaffolding_System SHALL track hint usage per session for analytics
6. THE Scaffolding_System SHALL reset hint progression at the start of each new turn

### Requirement 14: Backward Compatibility with Existing Sessions

**User Story:** As a system administrator, I want existing sessions to continue working, so that learners don't experience disruption during the migration.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL support both Claude 3 Haiku and Amazon Nova Pro models simultaneously
2. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL determine model selection based on a feature flag configuration
3. WHEN the feature flag is disabled, THE Conversation_Engine SHALL use Claude 3 Haiku with existing prompt format
4. WHEN the feature flag is enabled, THE Conversation_Engine SHALL use Amazon Nova Pro with optimized prompt format
5. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL maintain identical response data structures regardless of model used
6. THE Conversation_Engine SHALL handle model-specific errors gracefully and fall back to the alternative model if available

### Requirement 15: Feedback Example Formatting

**User Story:** As a learner, I want to see specific examples of my mistakes, so that I understand exactly what to improve.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Feedback_Formatter SHALL identify 2-3 specific learner utterances that contain errors
2. THE Feedback_Formatter SHALL format each example as: "Bạn nói: [original] → Nên nói: [correction]"
3. THE Feedback_Formatter SHALL provide brief explanations for each correction in Vietnamese
4. THE Feedback_Formatter SHALL prioritize high-impact errors (grammar, vocabulary) over minor pronunciation issues
5. THE Feedback_Formatter SHALL avoid overwhelming learners by limiting examples to 3 maximum per session

## Success Metrics

### Performance Metrics
- **Response Latency (TTFT)**: < 400ms for 95th percentile
- **Total Response Time**: < 2 seconds for 95th percentile
- **Model Availability**: > 99.5% uptime
- **Streaming Success Rate**: > 98% of responses delivered via streaming

### Quality Metrics
- **Response Length Compliance**: > 95% of responses within token limits for each proficiency level
- **Format Compliance**: > 99% of responses without markdown or structured formatting
- **Guardrail Effectiveness**: < 5% of conversations require multiple redirects for off-topic behavior

### User Experience Metrics
- **Session Completion Rate**: > 80% of started sessions completed (up from current baseline)
- **User Satisfaction Score**: > 4.2/5.0 average rating (up from current baseline)
- **Beginner Retention**: > 70% of A1-A2 learners complete 3+ sessions in first week
- **Hint Usage Rate**: 30-50% of A1-A2 sessions use at least one hint (indicates appropriate scaffolding)

### Business Metrics
- **Cost per Conversation**: < $0.05 per session (target with Nova Pro migration)
- **Daily Active Users**: 20% increase within 30 days of launch
- **Average Session Duration**: > 5 minutes (indicates engaging conversations)
